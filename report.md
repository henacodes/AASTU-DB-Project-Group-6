1.1 Background of the organization 
Ride Meter Taxi is a meter taxi service company operating in Addis Ababa, providing transportation services to passengers across the city. The company manages a network of registered drivers and vehicles, where fares are calculated using a meter based on distance traveled and time spent during trips. 
The operation of such a service involves managing passengers, drivers, ride requests, trip records, and fare calculations. Efficient handling of this information is important for maintaining organized operations and supporting service delivery. 
A database system can be used to store and manage this data in a structured way, enabling better organization, retrieval, and analysis of operational information.
1.2 problem statement 
Meter taxi services involve multiple activities such as managing passenger requests, assigning drivers, recording trip details, and calculating fares. Without a structured database system, managing this information can become difficult to organize. 
In particular, the absence of a well-designed database may limit the ability to:
store and retrieve ride service related data efficiently
track trip details such as time, distance, and status
manage driver availability in an organized manner
generate reports for analysis such as ride frequency or usage patterns
Therefore, there is a need to design a database system that can support efficient data management, structured storage, and basic analytical capabilities for a meter taxi service.
